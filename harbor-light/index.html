What we do

Three ways we keep tables full

Every program is free, walk-in friendly and staffed by neighbors. No paperwork beyond your first name and zip code.

Choice Pantry

Shop our shelves like a corner market — fresh produce, dairy and staples, chosen by you. Tue, Thu & Sat.

Community Supper

A hot, sit-down dinner every weeknight at 5:30. Everyone eats together; nobody is asked why they came.

Weekend Backpacks

Kid-friendly food bags sent home every Friday through 14 partner schools, so Monday doesn't start hungry.
