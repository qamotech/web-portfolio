Questions, answered

Do I need to register to get food?

No. Bring yourself and a bag. We ask for a first name and zip code so we can plan stock — that's it.

Can kids volunteer?

Ages 12+ can volunteer with a parent. Teens 16+ can volunteer on their own with a signed permission form.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Harbor Light would be a registered 501(c)(3), and you'd receive an emailed receipt for every gift. (This is a fictional sample organization.)

Do you accept food donations?

Yes — shelf-stable, unexpired items Tue–Sat. We can't accept home-cooked food or opened packages.

Can my company volunteer as a team?

Absolutely. Groups of 6–15 can book the Thursday evening packing shift. Email us to reserve a date.
